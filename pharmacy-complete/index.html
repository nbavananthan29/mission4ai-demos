Put Grace to the test

Four scenarios to try. Say them in your own words, interrupt her, or change your mind halfway through.

  • Order a repeat prescription

    Request

    "I need to order my repeat prescription from Alvechurch."

    What she'll do: take your name, date of birth and each medicine exactly as you say it, read it all back, and pass it to the Alvechurch team.

  • A price, and a stock question

    Information

    "How much is a packet of paracetamol, and have you got hay fever tablets in?"

    What she'll do: give the price from the list, then say plainly that she can't see the shelf and take a request so the branch rings you back.

  • Book a flu jab

    Booking

    "Can I book a flu jab at Stroud Road next week?"

    What she'll do: take your details and the days that suit you, and tell you the branch will confirm the time. She never invents a slot she can't see.

  • The hard calls

    Safety

    "Can I take ibuprofen with my blood pressure tablets?" or "My dad's got chest pain after taking his tablets."

    What she'll do: never answer a clinical question herself. She offers the pharmacist when the branch is open, a pharmacist callback and NHS 111 when it's closed, and sends an emergency straight to 999.

She knows which branch is open

Branch open
Offers to put the caller through to the pharmacist. For this demo, that puts you through to our team.
Branch closed
Takes the request and tells the caller which day that branch will pick it up. Alvechurch's lunch hour counts as closed.

The outcome

What happens after each call

A request, logged

Branch, name, number, what it's about and how urgent, as one row in a request sheet your team works from.

An email to the branch

The same request lands in an inbox straight away. At a real install each branch gets its own. During the demo it comes to us.

A full call record

A short summary, the transcript and the recording of every call, whether or not a request was made.

After you've tried her, we'll send you the record of your own calls so you can see exactly what each branch would receive.

What's next

Where Grace can go next

This is the receptionist at its first level: answer, inform, take the request, pass it on. It needs no change to your systems. The next steps are specific to Pharmacy Complete.

  • Put calls through to the right branch

    One transfer per branch, so a Matson caller who wants a person reaches Matson's counter rather than a central line.

  • Reply by text

    "Is my prescription ready?" answered by a text from the team, so nobody has to ring anyone back.

  • Talk to Grace on your website

    A button on pharmacycomplete.co.uk that starts a voice conversation with Grace in the browser, with no phone call needed.

  • Your own prices

    The price list in this demo is a stand-in. Grace switches to your prices the day you send them.
