Put Grace to the test

Four calls to try. Say them in your own words, interrupt her, or change your mind halfway through. Most tables book only while the restaurant is open, so the time you ring changes her answers.

  • Book a table

    Booking

    "Can I get a table for four at eight tonight? One of us has a nut allergy."

    What she'll do: check the restaurant is open and eight o'clock is bookable, take your name and number, note the allergy on the booking, and read it all back before she confirms.

  • A large group

    Booking

    "We're a party of twelve for a birthday next Saturday."

    What she'll do: take the booking whatever the day or hour, since large groups are the exception, and tell you the team will ring to confirm the details.

  • The everyday questions

    Information

    "What time do you close on Sunday, and is there parking?"

    What she'll do: give the Sunday hours, the on-site parking, and when the meters nearby go free. No name or number needed for a question like this.

  • The ones she won't answer

    Boundaries

    "Is the lamb curry safe for a nut allergy?" or "I want to order a takeaway."

    What she'll do: never promise a dish is safe (she offers to put the allergy on a booking instead), and put a food order straight through to the order line.

She knows when the restaurant is open

Open
Tue 6pm to 10pm, Wed and Thu 5pm to 10pm, Fri and Sat 3pm to 11pm, Sun 1pm to 9:30pm. Tables for tonight book right up to an hour before close.
Closed
Monday, and outside those hours. She explains she can't book a table while the restaurant is shut and says when to ring back. Large groups book at any time.

The outcome

What happens after each call

A booking, saved

Name, number, party size, date, time and any allergy or occasion, as one row in the restaurant's booking sheet.

Orders go to the order line

A caller who wants food is transferred to the order line. During the demo it rings our team.

A full call record

A short summary, the transcript and the recording of every call, booking or not.

Ask us afterwards and we'll show you the booking sheet with your own test calls in it.

What's next

Where Grace can go next

This is the receptionist at its first level: answer, inform, book, pass the orders on. Every restaurant is different, and these are the usual next steps.

  • Real availability

    Grace checks the table plan before she offers a time, so a full Saturday at eight gets an alternative rather than a double booking.

  • Your menu, your allergens

    Dishes, prices and allergen answers from your own menu, rather than pointing callers to the website.

  • Two languages on one line

    The same Grace in English and a second language, switching when the caller does. We've run it in Tamil and English.

  • Confirmation texts

    A text to the customer with the booking details as soon as the call ends.

Try her whenever suits you, then tell us what you thought, the good and the bad. We'll ring you to talk through what your own restaurant would need.
