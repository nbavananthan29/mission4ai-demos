If you smell gas

A smell of gas. Please, straight away: don't use switches or flames, open doors and windows, leave the property, and ring the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. Then ring us and we'll help with whatever follows.

The same for a carbon monoxide alarm: get out, get fresh air, ring 0800 111 999, and 999 if anyone feels unwell.
