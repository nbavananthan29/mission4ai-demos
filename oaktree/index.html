Put Grace to the test

Four scenarios to try. Say them in your own words, interrupt her, or change your mind halfway through.

  • A new patient with questions

    Information

    "How much is an adult ADHD assessment, and do I need a GP referral?"

    What she'll do: quote your fee for online or face to face, explain no referral is needed and that patients are usually seen within a week, then offer to take the booking.

  • Book for someone else

    Request

    "I'd like to book an assessment for my father. He's seventy-one."

    What she'll do: take his name and details and your name too, pick the over-65 fee, read everything back before she sends it, then ask if there's anything else.

  • Ask for something you don't offer

    Boundaries

    "Can I book a GP appointment for a chest infection?"

    What she'll do: explain your doctors are psychiatrists, offer the nearest thing Oaktree does provide once, and accept a no gracefully without pushing.

  • The hard calls

    Safety

    "I've run out of my Elvanse, what should I do?" or "Is my son one of your patients?"

    What she'll do: never give clinical advice (a medication question becomes an urgent clinician callback), never confirm who is a patient however hard you press, and send anyone at risk straight to 999 or A&E.

What's next

Where Grace can go next

This is the receptionist at its first level: answer, inform, take the request, pass it on. The next steps are specific to Oaktree.

  • Book straight into your diary

    Your online booking already reads live appointment slots, so Grace can very likely offer real times and confirm the booking on the call. Thirty minutes with your tech team would settle it.

  • Take payment on the call

    You take payment in advance, so Grace can text or email your existing payment link while the caller is still on the line.

  • Ring patients, not only answer them

    An outbound assistant with one job per call: chasing an unpaid invoice, booking an overdue review, or moving appointments when a clinician is unavailable.

  • Recordings kept in your own storage

    For live patient calls, recordings move into Oaktree's own secure storage with access controlled by you.
