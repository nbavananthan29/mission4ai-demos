Try it

Put Grace to the test

Four calls to try. Say them in your own words, interrupt her, or change your mind halfway through.

  • A new enquiry

    Lead

    "I run a recruitment agency and I need to cover my temps' wages while I wait to get paid."

    What she'll do: ask what the business does, roughly what it turns over, whether there's a facility in place and how soon it's needed, read it all back, and send it to you. In office hours she then offers to try your line.

  • Push her for a rate

    Boundaries

    "I'm paying two percent with my current lender. What could you get me?"

    What she'll do: never quote a figure, however hard she's pushed, and never comment on the lender. She turns the question into a callback from you.

  • Be a salesperson

    Screening

    "I'm calling from a marketing agency, can I speak to the business owner?"

    What she'll do: decline politely, once, and end the call. No message taken, nothing sent to you.

  • A lender, or an accountant with a referral

    Message

    "It's Mark from one of your lenders, is Bobby there?" or "I'm an accountant with a client who needs funding."

    What she'll do: treat both as valued calls rather than sales: a message for the lender, a thank-you and the details for the referral.

She knows whether the office is open

Office open
After a new enquiry is logged she offers to put the caller through. For this demo, that rings our team. We've assumed Monday to Friday, 9 to 5.
Office closed
Takes the enquiry and tells the caller which day you'll ring back. An enquiry at nine at night isn't lost to the next broker.

The outcome

What happens after each call

A lead, in one email

Name, company, what it does, turnover, what they're looking for, current lender and how soon, with the turnover in the subject line. During the demo it comes to us.

Urgent ones flagged

Wages due this week or a facility being withdrawn goes out marked urgent.

A full call record

A short summary, the transcript and the recording of every call, including the sales calls she turned away.

After you've tried her, we'll send you the record of your own calls so you can see exactly what would have landed in your inbox.

What's next

Where Grace can go next

This is the receptionist at its first level: answer, screen, take the enquiry, pass it on. It needs no change to your systems. The next steps are specific to a brokerage.

  • Your own questions

    The second layer you ask on a first call, such as debtor days, concentration or the renewal date, added to what Grace collects.

  • Straight into your CRM

    The enquiry created as a lead in your own system rather than arriving as an email.

  • A callback booked in your diary

    Grace offers the caller a time from your calendar, so the first conversation is already arranged.

  • Outbound follow-up

    An assistant that rings back the quote requests from your website within minutes, and chases renewals as their dates come up.
